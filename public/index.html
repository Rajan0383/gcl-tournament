📋 Tournament Rules

🏏 Basic Rules

  • Teams: 7 vs 7 (Max 9 players per squad)
  • Overs: 4 overs per innings

🔥 Over 1: LBW OVER

Numbers: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6

LBW Rule:

If the difference is exactly 1 for three consecutive balls, the batsman is OUT (LBW).

⚡ Overs 2-3: NORMAL OVER

Numbers: 3, 4, 5, 6

Wide Ball:

3 vs 6 OR 6 vs 3 → WIDE

No-Ball + Free Hit:

Batsman = 5 + any guess other than 5 → NO-BALL

💥 Over 4: POWERPLAY OVER

Numbers: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6

Wrong Guess:

➜ Batsman's number is DOUBLED

Correct Guess:

➜ Batsman is OUT and runs DEDUCTED

📌 Common Rules

  • Discipline: Respect all players
  • Fair Play: No cheating
  • Host Decision: Final
  • PM System: Batsman sends score via PM
  • Public Guess: Bowler announces in main room
