📋 Tournament Rules

🏏 Basic Rules

  • Teams: 7 vs 7 (Max 9 players per squad)
  • Overs: 4 overs per innings
  • Player Types: Manager, Captain, Vice Captain, RTM, Auction Players

🔥 Over 1: LBW OVER

Numbers: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6

No wide/no-ball rule in this over.

LBW Rule:

If the difference between the batsman's number and bowler's guess is exactly 1 for three consecutive balls, the batsman is OUT (LBW).

Example:
Ball 1: Batsman 4 → Bowler 5 (Diff = 1)
Ball 2: Batsman 3 → Bowler 4 (Diff = 1)
Ball 3: Batsman 6 → Bowler 5 (Diff = 1)
✅ LBW OUT!

⚠️ If any ball has a difference not equal to 1, the LBW count resets to zero.

🎯 Exact guess (same number) = Normal Wicket (breaks LBW sequence)

⚡ Overs 2-3: NORMAL OVER

Numbers: 3, 4, 5, 6

Wide Ball:

3 vs 6 OR 6 vs 3 → WIDE

➜ Batsman's number added as runs, ball repeated

No-Ball + Free Hit:

Batsman = 5 + any guess other than 5 → NO-BALL

➜ 5 runs added, next ball is FREE HIT

Free Hit:

Only 4 or 6 count as runs

Correct guess = Dot Ball (no wicket)

💥 Over 4: POWERPLAY OVER

Numbers: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6

If Bowler Guesses WRONG:

➜ Batsman's number is DOUBLED and added to team score

Example: Batsman = 5, Bowler = 4 → +10 runs

If Bowler Guesses CORRECT:

➜ Batsman is OUT

➜ Batsman's number is DEDUCTED from team score

Example: Score = 42, Batsman = 6, Bowler = 6 → 42 − 6 = 36

📌 Common Rules

  • Discipline: Respect all players and the host
  • Fair Play: No cheating or manipulation
  • Host Decision: Host's decision is final
  • Timing: Be on time for your matches
  • Communication: Use PalTalk room for all communication
  • PM System: Batsman sends score via PM to host
  • Public Guess: Bowler announces guess in main room

🔄 Double Batting & Strike Rules

Double Batting Game:

Both batsmen are active on the field simultaneously.

Each batsman takes turns facing the bowler.

Strike Change on Odd Numbers:

When the batsman scores an ODD number (1, 3, 5), the strike changes.

When the batsman scores an EVEN number (2, 4, 6), the strike remains.

Example:
Batsman scores 3 → Strike Changes (Odd number)
Batsman scores 4 → Strike Remains (Even number)
Batsman scores 5 → Strike Changes (Odd number)
