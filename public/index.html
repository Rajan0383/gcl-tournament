📖 About the Tournament

Welcome to the GEM-STAR Championship 2026 – the most exciting PalTalk cricket tournament! This is a 7 vs 7 cricket competition where teams battle it out in 4-over matches with the 3-4-5-6 wide system.

Each team consists of 9 players including Captain, Vice Captain, and squad players. The tournament features real-time score tracking, player statistics, and a live points table.

📋 Tournament Rules & Regulations

G3M-STAR CHAMPIONSHIP 2026

🏏

RULE 1 — TEAM COMPOSITION

  • 7 vs 7 format
  • Maximum 9 players per squad
  • Every player must put their official team logo in their display picture (DP).
🏏

RULE 2 — MATCH PLAYING RULES

  • 4 overs per side
  • Numbers: 3, 4, 5, 6
  • Wide: 3 vs 6 OR 6 vs 3 = WIDE
  • No-Ball: 1 No-Ball on 5
  • Double-Batting Format
  • Strike will change on 3 and 5
⏰

RULE 3 — MATCH TIMING & WALKOVER

  • Match Start: 9:30 PM IST
  • Walkover Deadline: 10:30 PM IST
  • Any team failing to join the room by 10:30 PM will be awarded a walkover against them.
⚠️

RULE 4 — PLAYER VERIFICATION

  • Every new player must be verified by the organizer.
  • Only verified players will be added to the official auction list.
  • No unverified player will be permitted to participate in the tournament.
⚠️

RULE 5 — PENALTY RULES FOR BATSMAN

🚫

1. Sending Score Without Host Permission

Batsman must not send their number to Host PM unless specifically requested.

First Offence → Warning + 3 runs DEDUCTED from team score
Second Offence → 6 runs DEDUCTED from team score
Third Offence → BATSMAN DISMISSAL
🚫

2. Sending Text Message Instead of Score

If a batsman sends a text message to host instead of a score:

First Offence → Considered as a DOT BALL
Second Offence → BATSMAN DISMISSAL
🚫

3. Sending Double Score (Same or Different Numbers)

If a batsman sends two scores (same or different numbers) in one PM:

First Offence → Warning + 3 runs DEDUCTED
Second Offence → 6 runs DEDUCTED + BATSMAN DISMISSAL
🚫

4. Editing or Deleting Score in Host PM

If a batsman is found to edit or delete their score in the host PM, they will be declared OUT immediately.

⚠️

RULE 6 — PENALTY RULES FOR BOWLER

🚫

1. Guessing Before Host Permission

Bowler must not guess or announce their guess before the batsman's score is set. The bowler must only guess when the host asks them to guess.

First Offence → Warning + 3 runs awarded to batting team
Second Offence → 6 runs awarded to batting team
Third Offence → 6 runs awarded to batting team + BOWLER DISMISSAL → New bowler will continue the remaining over
🏟️

RULE 7 — ORGANIZER'S AUTHORITY

  • The organizers will enforce all rules and regulations mentioned in this rulebook.
  • ⚠️ The organizers reserve the right to modify, amend, add, or change any rule depending on the situation and circumstances of the tournament.
  • Any decision taken by the organizers for maintaining the smooth operation and fairness of the tournament will be considered FINAL.
